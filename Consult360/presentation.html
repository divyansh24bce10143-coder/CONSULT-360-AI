Our Solution

Meet Consult360 AI

A doctor-facing AI copilot that ingests any patient PDF report and generates a structured, scannable pre-consultation brief in under 10 seconds — with explainable risk flags, timeline, and missing test alerts.

Gemini Summary
Step 1–2
PDF → OCR
PDF.js extracts text client-side. Tesseract.js as OCR fallback for scanned images.
Step 3
Entity Extraction
Gemini extracts diagnoses, medications, vitals, labs, symptoms, allergies into structured JSON.
Step 4–5
Timeline + Changes
Chronological event timeline. Detects clinical changes across visits (↑ worsening / ↓ improving).
Step 6–7
Risk + Summary
Explainable risk flags with evidence trails. Gemini generates TL;DR + narrative + action items.

Urine Protein: Trace  |  Creatinine: 1.1 mg/dL (↑22%)  |  Ankle edema (new symptom)
